If Ricky is the right broker for you, the next step is straightforward.

  1. 01He sends your personalized shortlist — 3 to 5 properties that match what you discussed, delivered within 2–5 business days.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to be in Puerto Rico to start?+

No. The first conversation — booking, call, and shortlist — is fully remote. You fly only when you're ready to see specific properties in person.

How long does the whole process take?+

From first call to closing: typically 3 to 6 months, depending on your needs. Most buyers take 1 to 3 trips to the island.

Why a call instead of just sending me listings?+

Because a 15-minute conversation saves you weeks of browsing. Ricky uses the call to narrow down area, budget, and property type — and only then sends a shortlist that actually matches.

What if I'm not ready to commit to anything?+

That's exactly what the call is for. Ricky will tell you honestly whether now is the right time, and what to do if it's not. No pressure.

Does Ricky help with financing?+

He connects you with lenders who work with mainland buyers. He does not lend money himself.

Does Ricky work with attorneys?+

Yes. He refers you to real estate attorneys he has worked with for title, closing, and other legal steps. He does not provide legal advice.

Can I get a mortgage in Puerto Rico as a mainland U.S. buyer?+

Yes — Puerto Rico is a U.S. territory, so U.S. citizens can access conventional financing, FHA loans, and VA loans on the island. Ricky connects you with lenders who specialize in working with mainland buyers.

What languages does Ricky speak?+

English and Spanish.

Where exactly does Ricky work?+

Primarily Dorado, San Juan, Condado, Isla Verde, and Humacao. He can also help in other areas of the island.

What is the commission?+

3% or less, depending on the purchase price. Disclosed in the Representation Agreement before any work begins.
