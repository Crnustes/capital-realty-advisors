The facts, not the noise

Four relocation myths
holding investors back.

01

You lose your United States citizenship or civil rights.

Puerto Rico is a United States territory. Establishing residency here does not require giving up your citizenship, your passport, or your rights under the American legal system.

02

You must abandon your mainland businesses and lifestyle.

With clear advisory guidance, physical residency rules are entirely manageable. You do not leave your current life behind; you simply extend your footprint to the island.

03

You must purchase a residence before submitting your application.

You do not need to buy a home before applying. The tax rules give you up to 2 years after your decree is approved to purchase a qualifying residential property.

04

Recent Act 38 amendments eliminated the benefits.

The core incentives remain fully active. However, your filing date determines whether your personal passive income is taxed at 0% under Track 1 or 4% under Track 2.

What is the importance of the December 31, 2026 deadline?+

The December 31, 2026 deadline is the statutory cutoff to submit an Act 60 decree application to the DDEC under Track 1, which secures a 0% tax rate on personal passive income and federal capital gains through 2035.

What changes if I apply after January 1, 2027?+

Applying after January 1, 2027 places your Act 60 decree under Track 2 rules, which increases the personal passive income tax rate from 0% to 4% through 2050 and introduces additional eligibility restrictions.

Does Capital Realty Advisors handle annual tax filings directly?+

Capital Realty Advisors does not file tax returns directly; instead, the firm manages your overall compliance strategy by tracking residency days and donation obligations while coordinating with specialized CPAs who execute the filings.

How long does the legal preparation process take for an Act 60 tax decree application?+

The legal preparation process for an Act 60 tax decree application typically takes 6 to 10 weeks due to attorney demand and DDEC documentation requirements, making early preparation essential ahead of statutory deadlines.

Is there a minimum purchase price for the Act 60 real estate requirement?+

There is no statutory minimum purchase price, though typical advisory clients acquire luxury homes ranging from $500,000 to over $3,000,000.

Are Act 60 property acquisitions restricted to specific areas in Puerto Rico?+

Act 60 property acquisitions are not restricted to specific geographic areas in Puerto Rico, allowing decree holders to purchase qualified residential property anywhere on the island, including Dorado, San Juan, Condado, Isla Verde, Humacao, and Rincón.

What is involved in annual Act 60 compliance?+

Annual Act 60 compliance involves maintaining physical presence records to prove 183 days of residency, completing a mandatory $10,000 annual charitable donation to qualified local non-profits, and filing an annual report with the DDEC.

Can I maintain my existing business entities in California or New York after moving under Act 60?+

Maintaining existing business entities in mainland states like California or New York is permitted after moving under Act 60, provided your corporate entities are properly structured by specialized CPAs to comply with federal income-sourcing regulations.

How does an Act 60 advisory firm differ from a standard real estate agent?+

An Act 60 advisory firm differs from a standard real estate agent by delivering an integrated consultancy framework—coordinating legal decree filings, aligning financial portfolios, managing luxury real estate acquisitions, and providing ongoing annual compliance oversight.
