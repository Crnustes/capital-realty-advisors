The facts, not the noise

Four relocation myths
holding investors back.

01

You lose United States citizenship or civil rights.

Puerto Rico is a United States territory. Establishing residency does not require relinquishing your citizenship, passport, or rights under the American legal system.

02

You must abandon mainland businesses and lifestyle.

With structured advisory planning, physical residency requirements are manageable. You do not abandon your current life; you extend your footprint to the island.

03

You must purchase a residence before applying.

The framework gives you up to two years after your decree is granted to purchase a qualified residential property.

04

Recent Act 38 amendments eliminated the benefits.

The incentives remain active. The essential question is how your filing timeline positions your personal passive income.

Frequently asked questions

Answers for a more
informed decision.

What changes if I apply after January 1, 2027?+

Applying after January 1, 2027 places your Act 60 decree under Track 2 rules, which increases the personal passive income tax rate from 0% to 4% through 2050 and introduces additional eligibility restrictions.

Does Capital Realty Advisors handle annual tax filings directly?+

We do not file tax returns directly. We manage your overall compliance strategy and coordinate with specialized CPAs who execute the filings.

How long does the legal preparation process take?+

The Act 60 tax decree application process typically takes 6 to 10 weeks due to attorney demand and DDEC documentation requirements.

Is there a minimum purchase price for the Act 60 real estate requirement?+

There is no statutory minimum purchase price, though typical advisory clients acquire luxury homes ranging from $500,000 to over $3,000,000.

What is involved in annual Act 60 compliance?+

Compliance involves maintaining physical-presence records, completing a mandatory annual charitable donation to qualified local nonprofits, and filing an annual report with the DDEC.
